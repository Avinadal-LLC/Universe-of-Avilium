Universe of Avilium

A living mythology of dynasties, forces, and the lives between them.

Content Notice

Content written and shared within the Avilium Universe may contain various triggers for people sensitive to dark, violent, sexual, and/or taboo topics.

The Avilium Universe is a living creative mythology spanning imperial dynasties, ancient energy systems, and characters whose lives have quietly shaped civilizations.

The year is 193 of the After-Fall calendar. The newly founded Avinadal Empire stands at the center of a fractured world — built by one man from grief and conviction, named after a ghost. At its edges, the energies of Myst and Malei continue their ancient tension: one the force of space, one the force of time, both running beneath the surface of every significant life in these records.

What you find here is the canonical archive of that universe — characters, lore, and the threads that bind them. More will be revealed.
