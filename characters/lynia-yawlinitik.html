Lynia Yawlinitik

canonical
Biography

Lynia Yawlinitik is a Plasidian woman, Queen of Yawlin, and High Imperial Consort of the Avinadal Empire — a title she took in exchange for the one she was born into and spent most of her life trying to escape. She and her sisters were raised by their father to be entertainers, trained in appearance, performance, and the subtler arts of managing powerful men. She learned pheromone manipulation and low-level hypnosis not as weapons but as armor: tools she developed to protect herself from a world where the men around her were controlling at best and monstrous at worst.

By the time she became Queen of Yawlin, she had turned those skills into a political style. When Aries Villanite arrived on Plasid, Ulmina identified what she was doing before Aries fully processed it, and his response was immediate and nearly fatal.

She agreed to ally with the rebellion anyway.

What changed her was not the near-death. It was what she saw of him afterward — his honesty, the weight he carried, the contradictions between his power and his vulnerability. During the first major battle against Edith Markov, she stepped in front of an attack meant for him, suffering a near-fatal wound in the process.

Afterward, she told him plainly that she wanted to give him the control she had spent her life protecting from others. He accepted.

Her relationship with Arielle developed separately and carries its own warmth. The two of them share more common ground than either of them initially expected.

Personality

Lynia Yawlinitik learned early that the safest way to navigate a world controlled by men was to understand them better than they understood themselves — their desires, their vanities, their pressure points. Pheromone manipulation and subtle hypnosis were tools of survival before they became tools of governance. By the time she was queen, the instinct was so embedded it operated almost automatically.

What she did not develop, alongside the armor, was the cruelty that usually accompanies it. The manipulation was protective, not predatory. She was not trying to take anything from anyone; she was trying to ensure that no one could take anything from her. This distinction matters for understanding why her reaction to Aries was so disorienting — he exposed the technique, nearly killed her for it, and she was surprised to find herself agreeing with him.

She is brave in a way she does not appear to have fully mapped onto herself. Stepping between Aries and an attack that could have killed her was not a calculated political move. It was a response, fast and unambiguous, to the recognition that she did not want him to be hurt. The self-awareness came after.

Her desire to serve Aries is genuine and also self-aware — she knows what she is offering, why she is offering it, and to whom. She chose him specifically because he is the first person in a position of power over her that she has believed, without reservation, is worth trusting with it.

Her relationship with Arielle gives her somewhere to put the warmth she spent most of her life keeping carefully managed. The two of them found each other without planning to.

Lore Records
Formation of the Avinadal Empire and the Plasid Campaign canonical ►
factionhistorypoliticswar

The Villanite Trading Company recruited Talia Hekata, a Hornhound dominatrix and sex therapist, as a mental health consultant. After a confrontation in which Aries shifted into beast form, Talia pledged loyalty to him and joined the team with a goal of building a capable imperial staff. Separately, Aries traveled to Plasid after Piper's crystal activated, where he learned of Empress Edith Markov's plan to transfer her consciousness into Enyxia Markov's body using a ritual that would absorb the memories of all Plasidian citizens. Trained in both Myst and Malei by Augustus Markov, Aries allied with Crowned Prince Vinisent Atril and Queen Lynia Yawlin to oppose Edith. The campaign involved liberating slaves in Harkul, surviving military assaults from Edith's forces, and the capture of Augustus and Apollo Regulus. Aries defeated Edith by using Piper's crystal to drive her from Enyxia's body, with Piper's essence expelling Edith before fading. Arielle killed Edith's physical body, trapping her in the Maleidic Realm. Following the victory, Aries and Enyxia founded the Avinadal Empire, named after Piper's middle name Avinadalia. Enyxia became Empress, and a formal imperial hierarchy was established encompassing the Regulus Sanguarchate and Plasidian Triumvirate. Lynia Yawlin and Arielle Villanite joined as High Imperial Consorts. Nyx Regulus and Aurelia Villanite also formed a romantic relationship during this period.

Founding of the Villanite Trading Company and Aries's Recovery Arc canonical ►
character-arcfactionhistorypolitics

Following a period of instability, Aries Villanite is summoned by Arielle Villanite to a derelict mansion in Napoli, where Apollo Regulus presents him with legal ownership of the newly formed Villanite Trading Company (VTC), a goods-and-services delivery organization. The VTC's founding client list includes the Waerrani Bounty Hunters' Guild, the Napolian Mercenary's Guild, the Regulus Sanguarchate, and Ensconce Technologies, granting it immediate legitimacy despite Napoli's notoriously strict Chamber of Commerce. Apollo manages administration with Arielle's support, while Nyx Regulus assumes leadership of the Regulus Sanguarchate aided by Aurelia Villanite. The mansion serves as both residence and headquarters. Aries initially resists the gesture through self-destructive behavior, culminating in a confrontation with Arielle that leads to a psychological breakthrough and his deeper involvement in VTC operations. He and Apollo develop a close personal relationship. Tamica joins as medical and biotech consultant, and following a long-delayed reconciliation with Kiriko, she joins as engineering and espionage consultant. Attempts to hire Trance Tauling are blocked by Napolian law prohibiting the hiring of executives from non-Napolian international firms. A legal caveat allows the hire if the executive is married to the company owner. Aries proposes marriage to Trance, who accepts, motivated primarily by personal affection for Aries and Lalith rather than professional interest. The marriage is formalized through paperwork only, with no ceremony.
