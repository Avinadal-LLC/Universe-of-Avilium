Rosalynia Yawlinitik

Plasidian Female canonical
Biography

Rosalynia Yawlinitik is a Plasidian woman, Queen of Yawlin, and High Imperial Consort of the Avinadal Empire — a title she took in exchange for the one she was born into and spent most of her life trying to escape. She and her sisters were raised by their father to be entertainers, trained in appearance, performance, and the subtler arts of managing powerful men. She learned pheromone manipulation and low-level hypnosis not as weapons but as armor: tools she developed to protect herself from a world where the men around her were controlling at best and monstrous at worst.

By the time she became Queen of Yawlin, she had turned those skills into a political style. When Aries Villanite arrived on Plasid, Ulmina identified what she was doing before Aries fully processed it, and his response was immediate and nearly fatal.

She agreed to ally with the rebellion anyway.

What changed her was not the near-death. It was what she saw of him afterward — his honesty, the weight he carried, the contradictions between his power and his vulnerability. During the first major battle against Edith Markov, she stepped in front of an attack meant for him, suffering a near-fatal wound in the process.

Afterward, she told him plainly that she wanted to give him the control she had spent her life protecting from others. He accepted.

Her relationship with Arielle developed separately and carries its own warmth. The two of them share more common ground than either of them initially expected.

Her relationship with Vynisian Atril emerged during the Plasid campaign and solidified after Avinadal's founding. They had recognized something familiar in each other almost immediately — both Plasidian, both shaped by courts that treated them as instruments before they treated them as people, both in love with Aries in ways that had not, at first, found their language. Their first language with each other was the worship they shared. The romance came later and arrived without ceremony. They have been a couple, openly and continuously, since shortly before the founding of the empire. Her relationship with Arielle continued unbroken across the transition; the three of them function as a poly arrangement in which Arielle is Rosalynia's own and the Lyn-Vyn partnership is the core. Aries moves through all of it as the figure they all orbit, neither absent nor possessed by any of them.

The biological wound Rosalynia carried into the empire was not one she advertised. Her father's training program had subjected all of his daughters to a sequence of forced procedures during their adolescence that left Rosalynia infertile in ways no Plasidian physician had been able to reverse. She had stopped expecting otherwise long before she met Aries. After her body was repeatedly exposed to Aries' DNA — through proximity, through intimacy, through the long cumulative effects of binding to a Maleidic-tolerant hybrid carrying every form of energetic signature in canonical record — her reproductive function began to return on its own. The healing was gradual and was first noticed by Alexandria during an unrelated medical workup. Rosalynia did not believe the result until she had ordered a second panel run.

The d'Villanite Bloodline was Rosalynia's idea. She brought it to Vynisian first, then to Aries. Aries received the proposal with the same quiet attention he tends to give Rosalynia's proposals; he did not object. The bloodline is structured around her and Vynisian as its founding generation. She bears children for both Vynisian and Apollo Regulus. Nyx Regulus, at Rosalynia's invitation and Vynisian's careful agreement, bears at least one of Vynisian's children to anchor the Regulus genetic line into the bloodline directly. The recruitment of adult d'Villanite members — Plasidians and others curated by Rosalynia and Vynisian for emotional and visual compatibility with Aries — runs in parallel with the biological work. Rosalynia treats both as one project. See [[d'Villanite Bloodline]].

Personality

Rosalynia Yawlinitik learned early that the safest way to navigate a world controlled by men was to understand them better than they understood themselves — their desires, their vanities, their pressure points. Pheromone manipulation and subtle hypnosis were tools of survival before they became tools of governance. By the time she was queen, the instinct was so embedded it operated almost automatically.

What she did not develop, alongside the armor, was the cruelty that usually accompanies it. The manipulation was protective, not predatory. She was not trying to take anything from anyone; she was trying to ensure that no one could take anything from her. This distinction matters for understanding why her reaction to Aries was so disorienting — he exposed the technique, nearly killed her for it, and she was surprised to find herself agreeing with him.

She is brave in a way she does not appear to have fully mapped onto herself. Stepping between Aries and an attack that could have killed her was not a calculated political move. It was a response, fast and unambiguous, to the recognition that she did not want him to be hurt. The self-awareness came after.

Her desire to serve Aries is genuine and also self-aware — she knows what she is offering, why she is offering it, and to whom. She chose him specifically because he is the first person in a position of power over her that she has believed, without reservation, is worth trusting with it.

Her relationship with Arielle gives her somewhere to put the warmth she spent most of her life keeping carefully managed. The two of them found each other without planning to.

Her relationship with Vynisian is the surprise. She did not expect to find, in the Crowned Prince of Atril, a man whose worship of Aries looked so much like her own that she recognized it on sight. She did not expect, having recognized it, to fall for him. She did not expect, having fallen for him, to be calmer with him than she has been with almost anyone. They are quiet together in ways neither of them has been with other partners. They run the bloodline together with the kind of administrative care most people reserve for nations.

The bloodline itself was her chosen expression of worship. Aries had a household, a marriage, an empire, and a daughter; he did not need her to bear his children. He needed something else from her, and she sat with the question long enough to figure out what it was. The answer she arrived at was: he needed a house. A family of people who had been raised, from birth or by deliberate adoption, to love him correctly. She decided to build him one. She is, by temperament, a builder. The bloodline is a queen's offering rather than a lover's gift, and she is at peace with the distinction.

Between 184 and 189 AF, Aries Villanite is established as the nominal owner of the Villanite Trading Company (VTC), a Napoli-based goods and services organization. The true architects of the venture are Arielle Villanite and Apollo Regulus, with covert backing from Mickail Sidson, whose seed capital, political connections, and Chamber of Commerce introductions made the company's rapid founding possible. Mickail's involvement was deliberately concealed to preserve his usefulness as a quiet operator within the Federation's blind spots. The VTC's founding client list includes the Waerrani Bounty Hunters' Guild, the Napolian Mercenary's Guild, the Regulus Sanguarchate, and Ensconce Technologies. The company operates out of a repurposed residential mansion in a commercial district of Napoli. Aries initially resists involvement due to guilt, but a confrontation with Arielle marks a turning point in his emotional state and he becomes an active participant. Apollo and Aries develop a close personal relationship. Tamica joins as a medical and biotech consultant. Following a long-delayed reconciliation, Kiriko Kuransagi joins as engineering and espionage consultant. Attempts to hire Trance Tauling are blocked by Napolian law prohibiting the hiring of executives from non-Napolian international firms without their resignation. A legal exception for spouses of company owners leads Aries to propose marriage to Trance, which she accepts. The marriage is conducted as a paperwork arrangement only, with Trance motivated primarily by personal affection rather than corporate necessity.

Villanite Trading Company: Founding and Early Years (184–189 AF) canonical ►
character-arcfactionhistorypolitics

Between 184 and 189 AF, the Villanite Trading Company (VTC) was established in Napoli through a coordinated effort by Arielle Villanite, Apollo Regulus, and silent patron Mickail Sidson. Aries Villanite was installed as the nominal owner and sole proprietor, while Apollo served as the public face to conceal Mickail's involvement. Mickail provided seed capital, political cover, Chamber of Commerce access, and the deed to a derelict mansion in a commercial district, which became the VTC's home and operational base. Founding clients included the Waerrani Bounty Hunters' Guild, the Napolian Mercenary's Guild, the Regulus Sanguarchate, and Ensconce Technologies. Arielle managed operations alongside Apollo. Aries initially avoided involvement due to guilt, but a confrontational breakdown with Arielle marked a turning point, after which he became actively engaged with VTC affairs and developed a close personal and sexual relationship with Apollo. Tamica joined as a medical and biotech consultant. Following a long-delayed reconciliation, Kiriko Kuransagi joined as engineering and espionage consultant. Attempts to hire Trance Tauling were blocked by Napolian law prohibiting the hiring of executives from non-Napolian international firms without resignation. A legal caveat allowed the hire if the executive married the company owner. Aries proposed to Trance during a board meeting; she accepted, motivated by personal affection rather than professional interest. The marriage was formalized through paperwork only, with no ceremony. Nyx Regulus assumed leadership of the Regulus Sanguarchate with Aurelia Villanite's assistance during this period.

Vynisian and Rosalynia: Recognition on the South Balcony draft ►
generatedplasidquietromance

On the south balcony of the Atril palace, following the conclusion of a war nearly lost, Vynisian Atril and Rosalynia Yawlinitik share an unguarded encounter that marks the beginning of a romantic and collaborative bond. Vynisian, caught in informal dress outside his private chambers, does not retreat when he finds Rosalynia watching the harbor. Both recognize in the other a deliberate division between public and private selves — a guarded court persona and a more authentic private one. Rosalynia reveals she deduced Vynisian's inner nature not from Katalyn's confidence but from observing how he managed Aries Villanite during their first meeting. Each acknowledges a long history of not forming romantic attachments; Rosalynia notes her only prior attachment was suppressed nearly forty years earlier and has only recently reactivated. They reach an implicit agreement to pursue a relationship structured around mutual trust and shared orbit around Aries, without displacing their existing loyalties to him. The arrangement is understood to be unconventional and is not expected to require external justification. The encounter concludes with Rosalynia inviting Vynisian to collaborate the following morning on a project: designing a house for Aries. The next morning, they begin planning what is described as the bloodline. This scene establishes the emotional and practical foundation of the Vynisian-Rosalynia relationship and its integration into the broader Aries-centered alliance structure.

Years 190–193 AF: The Plasidian Campaign and Rise of the Villanite Coalition canonical ►

Between 190–193 AF, Aries Villanite (age 30) expands the Villanite Trading Company by recruiting Talia Hekata, a Hornhound dominatrix and sex therapist, as mental health consultant. Talia submits to Aries after a confrontation triggers his beast form, thereafter dedicating herself to building an elite staff for him. A Plasidian delegation led by Augustus Markov, Crown Prince Vynisian Atril, and handler Katalyn Atrilmed arrives at VTC HQ requesting Aries' help to stop Edith Markov's ritual — a consciousness-transfer targeting Enyxia Markov's body, which would grant Edith control over all Plasidian memories. Aries agrees and travels to Plasid with Augustus and Apollo Regulus, receiving training in both Myst and Malei. The coalition secures alliances with Yawlin under Queen Rosalynia Yawlinitik and Atril, then liberates Harkul from a corrupt regime concealing a slave trade. Katalyn Atrilmed, manipulated by radical Atrilian Nationalists who were secretly poisoning Atril's king, attempts to assassinate Aries; Aries subdues her and executes her at Vynisian's reluctant direction. During the campaign against Edith's forces, Augustus and Apollo are captured. Aries defeats Edith by using Piper's crystal to expel her from Enyxia's body; Arielle kills Edith's physical form, trapping Edith in the Maleidic Realm. Enyxia, carrying the memories of all Plasidians and vast Mystic power, proposes assuming Edith's rule as a benevolent successor. Augustus recommends she rule jointly with Aries, whom Enyxia openly declares she loves.
