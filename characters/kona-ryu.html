Kona Ryu

canonical
Biography

Kona Ryu is a Hav woman whose name has passed from recorded history into legend, though the events of her life shaped the world in ways that continued for centuries after her death. She was the lover of Liu Dero, a Hadien whose growing signs of divine ascension attracted the attention of Hadieha, the Mystic of Darkness — and that attention changed everything.

Alifia, the Mystic of Creation, had blessed Kona with a gift called the Nesur: a manifestation of the potential for mortals to enter divinity. When Hadieha traced the trail of that divinity back to Alifia and executed Liu, Kona fled to Havimael and was hidden on Eitoria. She carried Liu's child for the duration of an extended stasis.

When she finally woke in Santerris, she gave birth to Arkem, clipped his wings, left him at an orphanage, and walked into a forest to die. She did not survive long enough to know what he became.

Her name persists primarily through the Nesur she carried and the bloodline she started by dying alone.

Personality

Kona Ryu the record preserves of Kona is mostly defined by what she did at the end: she gave birth to a child who would reshape the world, clipped his wings to protect him from being identified as her son, left him at an orphanage, and walked into a forest to die. These are not the actions of someone defeated. They are the actions of someone who had decided, with clarity, what the situation required and who was going to be the one to pay the cost.

She had carried the Nesur — Alifia's blessing, the potential for mortal divinity — and watched it attract the attention that destroyed everything she had. Liu was executed for what he was becoming. She survived by running. The extended stasis that followed was not a choice in any meaningful sense. She woke up alone, weakened, carrying a child whose existence made her a target and who carried that same dangerous potential.

She removed the wings. She left him somewhere he could grow without being found. She died.

This sequence suggests a personality organized around protection at personal cost — possibly a remnant of the Nesur, possibly simply who she was. The record does not give enough of her interior life to be certain. Kona Ryu it does give is a woman who understood the weight of what she carried, made the decision that protected the person she loved most, and did not wait for a more comfortable option.

Fall of the Mystics: The War of Creation and Its Aftermath (500–250 BF) canonical ►
historymagicreligionwar

In the primordial era, the supreme being Toriatik created the first Mystics to alleviate his loneliness, beginning with Alifia, the Mystic of Creation. His cruelty toward his creations caused Alifia great suffering, which influenced the birth of her children Havimael (Mystic of Light) and Hadieha (Mystic of Darkness). While Mystics typically created Mysterva — demi-divine beings dependent on their creators — Havimael created the Hav, a self-developing race, and Hadieha secretly created the rapidly evolving Hadien, both violating Toriatik's prohibition on independent growth. This triggered a war among the Mystics. Alifia granted a Hav named Kona Ryu a power called the Nesur, enabling mortals to ascend toward divinity. Kona's Hadien lover Liu Dero began showing signs of divine ascension as a result. Hadieha traced this back to Alifia, tortured her, apparently executed Liu, and planned to use Kona's unborn child — blessed by the Nesur — as a vessel to overthrow Toriatik. Kona fled to Havimael, who hid her on the neutral world Eitoria. During the final battle against Toriatik, Hadieha betrayed Havimael, sealing all Mystics within Eitoria and entering a deep sleep. Liu, who had survived, became the legendary monster known as the Black Devil before being defeated in the region later named Liundero. Kona awoke in Santerris, gave birth to Arkem, clipped his wings, left him at an orphanage, and died. Arkem's clipped wings remained with her.
