Aries Villanite

Hybrid Male canonical
Biography

Aries Villanite is a Hybrid male and Emperor of the Avinadal Empire, though that title tells little of the road that led him there. Born in 160 AF inside a secret laboratory beneath the Hadi Enterprises Maleidic Power Plant on Anili Island, Aries was never a child so much as a product — an experimental subject engineered for an extreme tolerance of Maleidic energy. His mother, Mariyan, was herself a subject. The records of the program named the Waerrani politician Tulian Mercusi as his biological father, chosen for the structure of his genetic code and rewarded handsomely for his contribution. The records were a lie. Mariyan had doctored them before her son was born; Aries' actual biological father is Idris Hadi himself. Aries did not learn this until decades later, on the perimeter of an arena.

He grew up alongside other facility children, forming bonds that the doctors used as leverage and tools. His closest childhood companions — Piper, a sickly but joyful girl, and Kiriko, a gifted fighter — were taken from him before he understood why. What he eventually learned was more devastating than their absence: both died because of him, during experiments that overloaded his Maleidic output and left no room for mercy. He had no memories of either death. The facility made sure of that.

When Mariyan passed, she left him a data drive. The truth it contained — about his origins, his father, the lies that had built their world — broke something open in him. He killed his way out of the facility in 175 AF — at fifteen years old, exactly fifteen years after the inaugural Annual War Games — destroyed the power plant's Maleidic core, and walked into a world that had no idea he existed.

The years that followed shaped him in quieter but no less brutal ways. He built a life on Kerbas Island alongside Robin Flynn and his best friend Kanin, doing real good in a city that needed it. When Kanin was murdered and Robin was lost to her own fear of him, Aries pivoted to something colder: he dismantled Tulian Mercusi's world piece by piece and then surrendered himself to arrest.

Prison sent him to the HECRC on Sharbain, where he was fitted with an experimental Maleidic nanosuit and found, instead of a tool, a partner — the AI that would become Ulmina. Their bond defined everything that came after. Piper, who had survived inside the facility all along, sacrificed herself to contain a reactor explosion engineered by Idris to push Aries onto the 20th Annual War Games roster — and Idris succeeded, because Aries had nothing left to refuse with. The Games themselves went sideways: a Federation ambush, a wing torn from Nyx Regulus's back, a public-feed declaration of a new nation, and a perimeter confrontation in which Idris finally revealed Aries' true paternity. Mickail Sidson of Napoli intervened, and Napoli took the surviving combatants — Aries, Apollo Regulus, Nyx, and Alexandria Zeru — into protective custody. Arielle and Aurelia were rescued shortly afterward from one of Idris' secret laboratories on Mickail's intel.

From there came the Villanite Trading Company, the Iron Maiden Mk. 2 suit, a paperwork marriage to Trance Tauling, and finally Plasid — where defeating Empress Edith Markov led to meeting Enyxia and the founding of the Avinadal Empire. He named it Avinadal after Piper's middle name.

He is known by many names — the Beast, Sire, DR-06N, V-1774-N1-T3. None of them quite cover what he is, which is exactly the point.

Personality

Aries Villanite is defined by contradiction. He is a caretaker who kills. A hero who calls himself a monster. A man with a hero complex so deep it was his first emotion, and a self-loathing so thorough it became his second. These two things do not resolve — they coexist, constantly, and everyone close to him lives somewhere in the space between them.

He is almost compulsively honest. Kanin noted it early: the honesty reads as kindness even when the content is brutal. He will tell you exactly what he is, what he did, and why — and then watch you decide what to do with it. He does not manage the impression he leaves. This is either a form of trust or a form of self-destruction, depending on the situation.

He dissociates easily, especially in crowds. The constant presence of others' memories in his mind makes it simple to step back and become an audience member. He developed a magnetic, charismatic persona during his time in Palin City — not because he is performative by nature, but because he studied the people around him and learned what worked. The persona is real, but it sits over something much quieter and darker.

He deflects emotional vulnerability with flirtation. If a conversation gets too close, his instinct is to redirect it sideways into something lighter or more physical. Ulmina and Alexandria both called him on this. He tends to comply when pushed, but the compliance costs him.

When he actually breaks — as he did with Arielle, screaming at her to hate him — what comes out is not anger so much as exhausted confusion. He does not understand why people stay. He has given them what he considers sufficient reason to leave, repeatedly, and they keep not leaving. He finds this disorienting. Arielle found this endearing and said so, which made it worse.

Sexually, he is dominant and voracious, but not indifferent. He pays attention. The hunger in him is real and he knows it; the care is also real and he sometimes seems surprised by it.

He is at his most dangerous when he is methodical. The beast form is terrifying, but it is predictable. The slow, deliberate dismantling of Tulian Mercusi's life over the course of a year — with no transformation, no spectacle, just patience and precision — was something different entirely.

He does not pray. But he keeps Piper's crystal on him at all times.

This story documents the first use of the mechanism: Everlette experiencing a severe behavioral episode, Aries cornering and deploying the override, and Everlette's chassis going peaceful upon contact.
