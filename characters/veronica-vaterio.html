Veronica Vaterio

Female canonical
Biography

Veronica Vaterio is a striking and athletic young woman whose appearance commands immediate attention, defined by her long white hair worn in a braid, vivid red eyes, and richly adorned figure. She favors a bold aesthetic of red and gold, dressing in a red brassiere with gold detailing, a low-riding gold mesh skirt, and ruby and gold jewelry that accentuates her tan, toned physique. Gold metal charms and bands complete her look, giving her the air of someone who treats her own body as both weapon and work of art.
